💬 Final Answer:

2.  Embeddings capture contextual meaning and similarity.
3.  Cross-encoders compare two texts for similarity.
4.  TensorFlow aids in building, training, and deploying models.

**Summary:** LLMs use embeddings and cross-encoders, supported by TensorFlow, for semantic search.
