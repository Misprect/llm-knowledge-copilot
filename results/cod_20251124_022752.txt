🧠 Question:
Why does giving the model a specific output format reduce hallucinations?

🎯 Strategy: cod

💬 Final Answer:
1.  **Structured Output:** Defining a specific output format guides the LLM.
2.  **Constraint Enforcement:** This format acts as a constraint, limiting free-form generation.
3.  **Reduced Ambiguity:** Clear structure minimizes the LLM's need to "guess" or invent.
4.  **Factual Grounding:** The format can prompt for specific, verifiable information.

**Summary:** Specific formats constrain LLMs, reducing invention and improving factual output.
